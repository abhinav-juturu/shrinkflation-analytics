Pricing Archetype Breakdown

Key Econometric Takeaways:

  • Silent Shrinkflation Dominance: Over 48% of SKUs belong to Quadrant II, where price was held completely static at ₹5 or ₹10 while grammage reduced by 15% to 25%.
  • Double Whammy Effect: 13.5% of products (e.g. Maggi, Dark Fantasy, Dove) implemented a two-stage strategy: first shedding 5-8% weight, followed by an overt ₹2–₹5 price hike in later waves.
  • Staples & Bulk Resilience: Standard bulk commodities (1kg salt, 5kg atta, 1L edible oils) stayed in Quadrant I due to Legal Metrology standard packaging mandates.

Business Insights & Regulatory Policy Framework

Actionable recommendations for FMCG leadership, consumer protection agencies (CCPA), and consumers.

⚖️

Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA)

  • Mandatory Dual Unit Pricing: Enforce prominent display of unit cost (₹ per 100g/ml) on front-of-pack and e-commerce listings alongside nominal MRP.
  • Mandatory Downsize Badge: Require a visible "Grammage Reduced by X%" badge for 90 days following any volume cut exceeding 3%.
  • Stricter Reformulation Disclosures: Prohibit masking downsizing behind "New & Improved Look" claims unless nutritional/active density has objectively increased.
🏢

FMCG Executive Strategy

  • Trust-Centric Transparent Pricing: Evidence indicates long-term brand equity erosion when consumers discover stealth cuts on social media. Transparent modest price hikes preserve loyalty better than deceptive downsizing.
  • Price Pack Architecture (PPA): Introduce differentiated tiered offerings (e.g. entry bridge pack at ₹7 or ₹12) instead of continuously decimating ₹5/₹10 core product grammage.
  • Value-Add Reformulation: If downsizing is inevitable due to commodity shocks, pair it with verifiable functional upgrades rather than cosmetic packaging illusions.
🛒

Consumer Vigilance Strategies

  • Focus on Unit Cost Over Pack MRP: Always compare ₹/100g across competing brands on quick-commerce apps rather than looking solely at the ₹10 or ₹20 price point.
  • Beware of Nitrogen Puffing: Chips and snack pouches often expand pouch volume using inert gas while reducing internal product grammage.
  • Crowdsourced Transparency: Report deceptive downsizing instances to consumer grievance portals and social transparency repositories.
